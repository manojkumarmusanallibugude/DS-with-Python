# 📧 Gmail Auto Forward Setup Guide

This guide explains how to automatically forward emails from a specific sender to another email address in Gmail.

---

## ✅ Step 1: Add Forwarding Address

1. Open Gmail
2. Click **Settings (⚙️)** → **See all settings**
3. Go to **Forwarding and POP/IMAP**
4. Click **Add a forwarding address**
5. Enter the destination email address
6. Click **Next** and confirm

---

## 🔐 Step 2: Verify Email Address

1. Open the inbox of the forwarding email
2. Find the verification email from Gmail
3. Click the verification link OR copy the code
4. Return to Gmail settings and confirm

---

## 🔁 Step 3: Enable Forwarding

1. In **Forwarding and POP/IMAP**
2. Select:
   - `Forward a copy of incoming mail to [your-email]`
3. Choose what to do with original emails:
   - Keep in inbox (recommended)
   - Archive
   - Delete
4. Click **Save Changes**

---

## 🎯 Step 4: Create Filter (Important)

1. Go to Gmail inbox
2. Click the filter icon in the search bar
3. In **From**, enter the sender's email (e.g., `example@gmail.com`)
4. Click **Create filter**
5. Select:
   - ✅ Forward it to → choose your email
6. Click **Create filter**

---

## 🚀 Result

- Emails from the specified sender will be automatically forwarded
- Other emails will remain unaffected

---

## ⚠️ Notes

- Forwarding works only after verification
- Emails marked as spam may not be forwarded
- Some institutions (college/work) may restrict forwarding

---

## 🧠 Example

| Sender Email        | Forward To                  |
|--------------------|---------------------------|

---

## ✅ Done!

You have successfully set up automatic email forwarding 🎉
